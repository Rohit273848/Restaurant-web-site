Privacy & Guest Data Policy

At L'Étoile Fine Dining, we respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting the guest data collected through our table reservation system and direct inquiry forms.

1. Information Collection

We collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and seating preferences exclusively when provided voluntarily to confirm table bookings or respond to concierge messages.

2. Data Utilization

Your information is used solely to facilitate your dining reservation, manage dietary allergy disclosures, and send booking reminders. We never sell or share guest records with third-party advertising networks.

3. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this policy or wish to request deletion of your booking records, contact our privacy officer at privacy@letoilerestaurant.com.
