Authentic Momo Story

Tibetan-Style Traditions & Exciting Fusion Flavors

Located at Shop No 6, near Dashmish Mandir, Jyoti Nagar, New Usmanpura, Sapna Momos is Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar's go-to destination for mouthwatering momos and fast food.

From traditional Tibetan-style Steamed Veg and Chicken Momos to innovative creations like extra crunchy Kurkure Momos, smoky Charcoal Tandoori Momos, creamy Afghani Malai Momos, and cheesy Pizza Momos, every plate is prepared fresh with rich dip sauces.
